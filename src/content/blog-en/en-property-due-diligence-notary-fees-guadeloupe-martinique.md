# Property Due Diligence and Notary Fees in French Overseas Territories

Acquiring real estate under French civil law offers world-class investor protection through public notary escrow and registered title deeds.

## Breakdown of Notary Fees and Land Registration

For existing residential properties, overall acquisition costs represent approximately 7.5% to 8.5% of the purchase price.

### Essential Due Diligence Checklist
1. **Loi Littorale & 50 Pas Géométriques**: Checking whether the parcel is situated in full private ownership or long-term administrative leasehold.
2. **Soil and Cyclonic Compliance**: Checking subsoil geotechnical surveys (G1/G2) and hurricane building standards.
3. **Environmental Diagnostic Audits**: Mandatory termite, lead, asbestos, and energy efficiency performance documentation.

Working with an accredited notary ensures 100% clean title ownership before funds transfer.
