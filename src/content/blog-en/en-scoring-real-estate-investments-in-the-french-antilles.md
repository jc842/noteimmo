# Scoring Real Estate Investments: The Note Immo Rating Methodology

To assist institutional investors and private buyers, Note Immo has engineered a multidimensional scoring index ranging from AAA to CCC.

## Core Scoring Pillars

- **Liquidity Rating (Weight 30%)**: Average days on market, historical sales volume within a 2km radius.
- **Climate Resilience Index (Weight 25%)**: Elevation above sea level, storm surge distance, hurricane building grade.
- **Legal & Title Security (Weight 25%)**: Title clarity, lack of joint-ownership undivided probate deadlocks.
- **Net Rental Yield (Weight 20%)**: Long-term vs. seasonal tourist occupancy ratios.

Properties evaluated at A or above demonstrate outstanding resilience and above-average resale liquidity.
