# Caribbean Real Estate Valuation Indices: Trends and Price Multipliers

Evaluating property in the French West Indies requires an in-depth understanding of local micro-markets, building depreciation under tropical climates, and legal zoning constraints.

## Market Valuation Multipliers

Unlike metropolitan France, coastal island real estate values are driven heavily by elevation, sea views, cyclone-resistant concrete standards, and short-term tourism rental yield potential.

### Key Cap Rate Indicators (2025-2026)
- **High-End Seaside Villas**: 4.8% to 6.2% net yield.
- **Urban Apartments (Jarry, Pointe-à-Pitre, Fort-de-France)**: 6.5% to 8.1% gross yield.
- **Prime Coastal Plots**: Capital appreciation averaging +5.4% annually.

Professional investors utilize discounted cash flow models adjusted for local insurance premiums and specific European tax schemes.
